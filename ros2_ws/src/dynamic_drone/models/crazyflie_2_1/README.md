# Crazyflie 2.1 Gazebo model

Saved as `crazyflie_2_1`, with four rotors and the official Bitcraze Crazyflie
body/propeller meshes. Generated by `scripts/create_gazebo_mission_world.py`.

Upstream: https://github.com/bitcraze/crazyflie-simulation
Commit: `7e93752dbc803af2488c1db46bb79b3da55f5d8c`
Source: `simulator_files/gazebo/crazyflie/model.sdf`
Copyright (c) 2024 Bitcraze, MIT. The upstream license is preserved at
`vendor/crazyflie-simulation/LICENSE`; do not remove it when redistributing.

Adaptations: namespaced topics, 29 g total nominal bare-airframe mass (25.8 g
body + four 0.8 g rotor links), contact sensor, 1 ms physics step, initial
pose from the mission YAML, conservative velocity/attitude gains and idealized
drag. Meshes and inertia originate from the experimental upstream CF2 model;
they are not separately measured CF2.1 calibration. Product specification:
https://www.bitcraze.io/crazyflie-2-1/

The model origin is at the bottom of the body collision box. It starts at the
white takeoff pad `(0, 0, 0.022)` m (2 mm above the 20 mm pad), with +x forward. Flight height and odometry
refer to that model origin, not the center of mass 17.425 mm above it.

No AI/Flow deck payload, sensor noise or real firmware is simulated here.
This is a basic Gazebo physics/control model, **not** validated Crazyflie SIL
or a claim of real-flight performance. World geometry is still a demo arena.
Mesh URIs resolve to this workspace's upstream checkout; regenerate on another
computer. Never scale the model up merely to make it easier to see in the GUI.
